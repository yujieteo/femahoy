This case checks the stress intensity factor K_I of a centre crack in a steel plate in tension (units SI-mm). CalculiX has no output of K for a crack in the mesh. Thus the case gets K_I from the displacements of the crack face, as the chat does.

Source

H. Tada, P. C. Paris and G. R. Irwin, The Stress Analysis of Cracks Handbook, 3rd ed., ASME Press, 2000: a centre crack in a strip in tension. The formula is from H. Tada, "A note on the finite width corrections to the stress intensity factor", Engineering Fracture Mechanics 3 (1971) 345-347:

K_I = σ sqrt(π a) F(a/b), F = [1 - 0.025 (a/b)^2 + 0.06 (a/b)^4] sqrt(sec(π a / 2b))

Here 2a is the length of the crack, 2b is the width of the strip, and the strip has no end. Sources give the error of this formula as 0.1 % to 0.2 % for all a/b.

mesh.py writes a spider web mesh. 16 rays go from the tip to the edges of the plate. 8 rings go from L = 0.5 mm (a/50) to 5 mm, with a constant ratio. 8 more rings go from 5 mm to the edges. Ring 1 has 6-node triangles. The mid-side nodes of the 2 edges from the tip are at L/4 from the tip. This puts the singularity 1/sqrt(r) of the stress in the elements at the tip (R. S. K_I from the displacements

On the face of the crack, near the tip, the opening of 1 face is v = (4 K_I / E') sqrt(r / 2π), with E' = E / (1 - nu^2) in plane strain. On the edge of a quarter-point element, v(r) = v_T + (-3 v_T + 4 v_B - v_C) sqrt(r/L) + 2 (v_T - 2 v_B + v_C) r/L. T is the tip, B is the node at L/4 and C is the node at L. Here v_T = 0. The terms in sqrt(r) give:

K_I = c (4 v_B - v_C), c = (E' / 4) sqrt(2π / L)

This is the displacement method of C. F. Shih, H. G. de Lorenzi and M. D. German, International Journal of Fracture 12 (1976) 647-651. For L = 0.5 mm, c = 204513.9 MPa mm^-0.5.

The *EQUATION at the end of mesh.inp makes U2 of node 802 equal to c (4 v_B - v_C). Node 802 has no element. Thus *NODE PRINT of the set KI writes K_I in job.dat, and the check reads U2 of this set. The result is K_I = 1053.28 MPa mm^0.5, 0.19 % above the reference. The sum of the reactions on LIGAMENT is 5000 N = 100 MPa x 50 mm x 1 mm.

The keyword *CRACK PROPAGATION of ccx does not model the crack. It takes K from the stress of the part without the crack, with fixed shape factors. Thus this case does not use it.
