This case checks the steady tip temperature of an aluminium pin fin (units SI-mm).

Source

F. P. Incropera, D. P. DeWitt, T. L. Bergman and A. S. Lavine, Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer, 6th ed., Wiley, 2007, Table 3.4, case A (convection heat transfer at the tip). At the tip, θ(L) / θb = 1 / (cosh mL + (h / mk) sinh mL), with θ = T - T∞ and m = sqrt(h P / (k A)).

P = 0.04 m and A = 1e-4 m2. Thus m = sqrt(50 · 0.04 / (200 · 1e-4)) = 10 1/m, mL = 1 and h / mk = 0.025. T_tip = 20 + 80 / (cosh 1 + 0.025 sinh 1) = 70.8757 °C.

*FILM in ccx 2.23 refuses a surface name. Thus job.inp has 1 line E, Fn, 20.0, 0.05 for each element face of the surfaces TIP, Y0, Y1, Z0 and Z1.

The mean of the area is the integral of the order-2 temperature on the tip face, divided by the area. It changes by less than 0.0001 °C from size 10 to size 1.25. Thus the mesh error of the temperature is less than 0.0001 °C. The check gives the same weight to each node of TIP. The nodes on the edges of the face are colder than the centre, and a finer mesh has a smaller fraction of them. Thus the mean of the nodes increases to the mean of the area. With size 5 it is 0.006 °C lower. The mean of the area is 0.008 °C higher than the fin theory. This is the difference between the solid and the fin theory.

Tolerance

The tolerance is 0.1 °C (abs_tol = 0.1), 0.2 % of the 50.9 °C difference between the tip and the air. The fin theory has 1 temperature in each section. In the solid, the tip face changes from 70.84 °C at the corners to 70.90 °C at the centre: the Biot number h (t/2) / k is 0.00125. Thus the check is the mean of the tip face. The mean is 70.8776 °C (+0.002 °C) with a mesh of size 5 mm. It is the sum of 3 parts ("Mesh study"): the solid against the fin theory +0.008 °C, the mean of the nodes -0.006 °C and the mesh less than 0.0001 °C.

Chat run

The request asked for the temperature at the centre of the tip face in plain words. The chat used a mesh of size 2.5 mm (6695 nodes). It gave 70.904 °C (+0.029 °C) at the nodes near the centre of the tip. The run took 111 s and cost 0.50 USD.

The chat run found 2 gaps:

  1. The first solve stopped: ccx refused *FILM with the surface name (TIP, F, 20.0, 0.05). The chat wrote a script that lists the element faces. crates/fh/src/daemon/agent.md now has this fact.
  2. The 3D view showed no temperature, because the reader of the .frd file kept the name NDTEMP. The reader now gives NT, and the view shows NT first when the step has no stress and no displacement.
