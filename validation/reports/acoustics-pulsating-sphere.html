This case checks the acoustic path: the sound that a pulsating sphere radiates into air at 500 Hz. The reference is the analytical solution. Thus the tolerance comes only from the mesh error of the model.

Why this case

No other case of the suite has any of these items:

  • Acoustics: the Helmholtz equation for the sound pressure, solved with GetDP.
  • Radiation into an unbounded region, with an absorbing condition on the outer face.
  • Second-order node functions (BF_Node_2E) on a first-order mesh.
  • A check of a mean over a surface (the radiation impedance) and of a complex value at a point.

Sources

  • L. E. Kinsler, A. R. Frey, A. B. Coppens and J. V. Sanders, "Fundamentals of Acoustics", 4th edition, Wiley, 2000, ISBN 0-471-84789-5, section 7.1, "Radiation from a pulsating sphere". It gives the outgoing spherical wave of a sphere with a uniform normal velocity, and its radiation impedance.

The derivation below gives the same result, so that a reader can check it without the book. With the time factor exp(j omega t), the outgoing spherical wave is p = A exp(-j k r) / r, with k = omega / c. The linear Euler equation gives the radial velocity: j omega rho u = -dp/dr. On the sphere r = a, u = U. Thus:

p(r) = rho c U z (a / r) exp(-j k (r - a))
z = j ka / (1 + j ka) = (ka)^2 / (1 + (ka)^2) + j ka / (1 + (ka)^2)

z is the specific radiation impedance of the sphere: p on the sphere divided by rho c U. The same wave also gives dp/dr = -(j k + 1 / r) p at each r. This is the condition on the outer face of the model.

Model

The GetDP case is the folder 500hz: case.geo for gmsh and case.pro for GetDP. The units are SI, with lengths in m.

ItemValue
Regionthe air between the sphere r = a and the outer sphere r = R = 0.5 m, in the octant x, y, z >= 0
Equationdiv grad p + k^2 p = 0 in the air
Symmetry planesdn p = 0, the natural condition. It is a rigid wall, and the solution is symmetric about each plane

Outer spheredn p = -(j k + 1 / R) p. It is exact for the outgoing spherical wave, thus it reflects no part of the wave
Elementsfirst-order tetrahedra. The pressure has second-order node functions: BF_Node on the nodes and BF_Node_2E on the edges. Gauss rules of 15 points (tetrahedra) and 7 points (triangles)
Meshsize 10 mm at the sphere and 50 mm at the outer sphere: 13.7 elements in a wavelength. 2248 nodes, 9527 tetrahedra, 15356 unknowns
Outputz.txt: 1 row with the 4 values of the check. z is the integral of p on the sphere, divided by the area of the meshed sphere and by rho c U. p.pos and p_abs.pos: p and \

The .pos files have first-order elements, because the mesh is first-order. Thus fh run reads them, and the page shows them.

Mesh study

These commands, in the repository root, solve the case on another mesh in a scratch folder. -setnumber sets the mesh sizes of case.geo in m:

mkdir /tmp/sphere && cp validation/cases/acoustics-pulsating-sphere/500hz/case.* /tmp/sphere && cd /tmp/sphere
gmsh case.geo -3 -o case.msh -setnumber ha 0.007 -setnumber hR 0.035
getdp case.pro -msh case.msh -solve '#1' -pos '#1'

Not in the check

  • Other frequencies: the solution is the same function of ka at each frequency. 1 frequency with ka near 1 checks the resistance and the reactance with the same weight.
  • Structural modes or a structure that vibrates in air: CalculiX solves the modes of the structure. A coupled case needs the velocity of the structure on the surface, which this case gives as U.
