This case checks frictional contact with partial slip (stick-slip): a steel cylinder on a steel block, pushed down and then pulled to the side with less than the slip force (units SI-mm). The centre of the contact sticks, and its 2 edges slip. The checks are the largest contact pressure p0 and the shear traction q(0) at the centre of the contact.

Source

  • C. Cattaneo, "Sul contatto di due corpi elastici: distribuzione locale degli sforzi", Rendiconti dell'Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei 27 (1938), 342-348, 434-436 and 474-478.
  • K. L. Johnson, Contact Mechanics, Cambridge University Press, 1985: chapter 4 (the Hertz contact of 2 cylinders) and chapter 7 (tangential loading, the partial slip of Cattaneo and Mindlin).

2 long cylinders of the same material touch along a line, in plane strain. P is the normal force and Q is the tangential force, each for 1 mm of length. R is the relative radius (here a cylinder on a flat, thus R is the radius of the cylinder), and 1/E* = 2 (1 - nu²)/E.

  • Hertz: the contact half-width is a = (4 P R / (π E*))^(1/2). The pressure is p(x) = p0 (1 - x²/a²)^(1/2), with p0 = 2 P / (π a).
  • Cattaneo: Q increases from 0 to a value less than μ P. The zone |x| < c sticks, and the zones c < |x| < a slip.

The shear traction q is the sum of 2 elliptical distributions:

  • In the slip zones c < |x| < a: q(x) = μ p(x).
  • In the stick zone |x| < c: q(x) = μ p(x) - μ p0 (c/a) (1 - x²/c²)^(1/2).

On a half-plane, an elliptical traction b0 (1 - x²/b²)^(1/2) on |x| < b gives a surface strain that is linear in x, in |x| < b. The 2 parts of q thus give strains of the same size and opposite signs in |x| < c, and the 2 bodies do not slip there. The total of q is Q = μ P (1 - c²/a²). Thus c = a (1 - Q / (μ P))^(1/2) and q(0) = μ p0 (1 - c/a).

The 2 bodies have the same material. Thus the pressure causes no tangential slip, and the shear traction does not change the pressure.

The steps:

  1. Move the top of the pad down by 0.02 mm. This starts the contact.
  2. Release the top in y and apply P = 4500 N at once: 2250 N on each end of the top.
  3. Release the top in x and increase Q from 0 to 1012.5 N in increments of 0.05 of the step. P stays.

Q acts on the top of the pad, 20 mm above the contact. In step 3, a couple F = 20 Q / 24 = 843.75 N on the 2 ends of the top cancels the moment of Q about the centre of the contact. The theory has no moment about the centre of the contact. A first model had a top that could not turn. The pad then bent, and the contact moved to 1 side. In that model, q(0) was +0.15 %, -0.29 % and -1.19 % from the reference for parts of 20, 40 and 80 mm. With the couple, the contact is symmetric, and the size of the parts changes q(0) by 0.22 % or less (see the tolerance).

The checks are at the end of step 3:

  • p0: the largest contact pressure (CSTR, component 1) of all contact points.
  • q_centre: the mean of S12 at the 8 integration points of each of the 2 block elements at (0, 0) (set CENTRE). On the top face of the block, the traction is (S12, S22). Thus S12 = q at the contact. At (0, 0), dS12/dx = 0 and dS12/dy = 0. Thus the mean of the integration points is q(0), with an error of the second order in the element size.
