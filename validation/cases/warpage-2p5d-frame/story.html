Conclusion

The model gives a warpage of -0.1477 mm at the substrate corner at 60 °C. This is 8.8 % smaller than the moiré value of Tsai, Wang and Liu (2021), -0.162 mm. The tolerance is 13 %, the root sum square of the errors of the inputs and of the measurement (see "Tolerance"). Thus the difference is 68 % of the tolerance. A finer mesh gives a smaller warpage: with the 0.5 mm mesh, the difference is 10.2 % (see "Measured result").

At 60 °C, the strain gauges of the paper give -0.168 mm, and the FEM of the paper gives -0.162 mm. The 2 largest errors of the budget are the zero error of the moiré (9.8 %) and the unknown die size (7.5 %).

The check is at 60 °C only. At 25 °C and 260 °C, the warpage of the model with the 1 mm mesh is 24 % and 22 % smaller than the moiré value (see "Other results" and "Causes of the difference"). Thus this case does not validate the warpage at these temperatures.

The 3D view shows u3, the displacement out of the plane, of the quarter package after it cools from 140 °C to 60 °C. The deformation is 50 times the true size. The package becomes a dome, and the corner moves to -z. The warpage is u3 at the corner relative to the centre. The lowest point of the substrate is not the corner, but a point on the edge x = 27.5 mm at y = 16.5 mm. It is 0.0038 mm lower than the corner.
