Conclusion

The model gives a warpage of -0.1477 mm at the substrate corner at 60 °C. This is 8.8 % smaller than the moiré value of Tsai, Wang and Liu (2021), -0.162 mm. The tolerance is 13 %, the root sum square of the errors of the inputs and of the measurement (see "Tolerance").

At 60 °C, the strain gauges of the paper give -0.168 mm, and the FEM of the paper gives -0.162 mm. The 2 largest errors of the budget are the zero error of the moiré (9.8 %) and the unknown die size (7.5 %).

The check is at 60 °C only. At 25 °C and 260 °C, the warpage of the model with the 1 mm mesh is 24 % and 22 % smaller than the moiré value (see "Other results" and "Causes of the difference"). Thus this case does not validate the warpage at these temperatures.
