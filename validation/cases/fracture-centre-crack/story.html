Conclusion

The CalculiX model gives the stress intensity factor K_I of the centre crack 0.19 % above the formula of Tada (1971). The model gives K_I = 1053.28 MPa mm^0.5, and the formula gives 1051.27 MPa mm^0.5. The tolerance is 0.5 %.

CalculiX has no output of K for a crack in the mesh. Thus the model gets K_I from the opening of the crack face at the 2 nodes of the quarter-point element at the tip (see "K_I from the displacements"). The opening of each node near the tip gives a value of K_I too. At the node nearest the tip, r = 0.125 mm, it is 0.25 % below the formula. Farther from the tip, the higher terms of the field make it smaller.

The finer meshes of the mesh study give K_I 0.10 % to 0.16 % above the formula. An error of the method gives a larger difference. For example, E in place of E' gives -9 %.
