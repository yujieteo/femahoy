Conclusion

The model gives the effect of the flap with the correct sign and about the correct size. Each 10 degrees of flap down adds 0.56 to the lift coefficient of the model, and 0.44 to 0.50 to the lift of the test of Abbott and Greenberg (1939). The lift of the model is 7 % to 22 % above Table I of the test, and the moment of the airfoil with the flap up or down is 20 % to 28 % larger. The section "Tolerance" below gives the causes: the lift slope of the model is 0.106 to 0.111 per degree and the lift slope of the test is 0.094 to 0.100, and the boundary layer on the flap of the test was thicker.
