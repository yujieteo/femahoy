Conclusion

The model gives a warpage 3.2 % smaller than the measured warpage of Calabretta et al. (2021). For a temperature increase of 40 K, the end of the slice moves -0.02711245 mm, and the measured rate of 0.700 µm/K gives -0.028 mm. The tolerance is 12 %.

The solve agrees with the theory of the same inputs: the plate form of the bilayer curvature gives -0.0271253 mm, 0.05 % more. Thus the difference from the measurement does not come from the solve. The 2 largest errors of the budget are the accuracy of the moiré (7.6 %) and the silicon modulus of the paper (7.1 %). The linear model of the paper is also below the measurement: it gives 0.660 µm/K, and this model gives 0.678 µm/K.
