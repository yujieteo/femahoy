Conclusion

The S6 shell model gives the deflection of the middle of the free edge within 0.3 % of the published value. The model gives uz = -301.54 mm at TIP, and MacNeal and Harder (1985) give -302.4 mm. The difference is 0.86 mm, 29 % of the tolerance of 1 %.

A finer mesh moves the deflection toward the published value. With 5000 mm elements the error is -6.4 %, and with 625 mm elements it is -0.2 % (see "Result"). The case mesh of 1250 mm is between them, at -0.3 %.
